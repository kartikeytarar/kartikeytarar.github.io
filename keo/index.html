KEO

Your screen is a window into a small room. The part sits inside it.

  1. Move your mouse slowly (or press Head + hands in Chrome) to look around the part.
  2. Type or speak what you want: "design a desk lamp". Common objects load instantly from the Keo library; anything else is built by Claude when it is connected.
  3. Switch models, or drop in your own .stl or .step file. Press the Keo voice button, then just say "Keo" before a command.

Click a part to name it. Press Explode to pull the assembly apart. Press 3 for red-cyan 3D, C for camera view.

Calibrate real size

Hold a bank card (credit or debit, 85.6 mm wide) against the screen. Drag the slider until the grey bar is exactly as wide as the card. Real size will then be true to the millimetre on this screen.
